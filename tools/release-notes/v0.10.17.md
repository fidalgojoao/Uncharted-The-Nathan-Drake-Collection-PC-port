**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (the multiplayer image is relinked in a few seconds). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online (multiplayer helper v7.61)

- **New RPCN accounts can play online.** An account that never played Uncharted 3 on PS3 used to get an error at Play Online; it now starts with a fresh profile, as on PS3.
- **Friends, invites and presence over RPCN** (first version, being tested with PS3/RPCS3 players):
  - Invite to Party, the invites list, accepting an invite and Join Party are connected to RPCN and the community invite server.
  - Add Friend works in game, and the friends list follows RPCN (online/offline, requests).
  - Friends in Uncharted 3 show their lobby status instead of "Campaign".
- **More online details in boot.log** for invites, messages and presence, to help with reports.

## Multiplayer import

- **The UK Game of the Year edition (BCES01692) works too**, next to the EU (BCES01670) and US (BCUS99086) editions. All three have the same 1.19 code and game files; only the speech languages differ (the import uses English).
