**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Launcher

- **Automatic updates (new LAUNCHER tab):** choose Automatic or Manual. Automatic installs a new version by itself when no game or import is running, with its progress in the gold bar; Manual keeps the UPDATE button. The update check at start moved here too.
- **The three games are on the cover picture**, next to the title, on frosted glass. The tabs and pages moved up.
- **Frosted background:** the cover art, blurred and darkened, behind everything below the picture.
- **RPCN** always sits in the right column of the Multiplayer tabs. Add friend is laid out like the other fields.
- **Uncharted 2:** the "multiplayer update needed" bar only shows for an import made on this PC.
