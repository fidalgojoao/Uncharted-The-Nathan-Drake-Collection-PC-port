**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (the multiplayer image is relinked in a few seconds). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online (multiplayer helper v7.59)

- **The game signs in with the launcher's RPCN login.** Set up your account once in the launcher (Multiplayer tab, RPCN Setup): when you start Uncharted 3, the game signs in by itself, and Play Online shows your name, level with prestige, stats and clan tag right away. The in-game RPCN page is not needed and is hidden while the launcher's login is used.
  - The launcher signs itself out first (RPCN allows one sign-in per account) and signs back in when you quit.
  - Your login is passed to the game only for that start and is never written to the game's own settings file.
  - Without a login saved in the launcher, the in-game RPCN page works as before.
