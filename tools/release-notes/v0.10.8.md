**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. The changed maps are rebuilt in the background; nothing is imported again. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Maps

- **36 more multiplayer and co-op maps are now rebuilt exactly like the reference build**, end to end. That includes Chateau shade, Airport, Desert, Borneo co-op, Facility, Fort, Gompa, Graveyard, Highrise, London streets, Museum, Oasis, Old Quarter, Ruins, Syria, Temple, Train Wreck, Village, Warzone and Yemen. Some had visual differences or rare problems that came from the old rebuild; those are gone with it.
- About 15 maps still differ from the reference and are being worked on.
