**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online

- **"srv: raw login failed" / "no connection found" fixed.** Some settings files still had an old test login mode switched on; the multiplayer helper (v7.57) now always uses the normal login and removes that setting the next time it saves.
- **Sign-in status in boot.log.** Every change of the online status is written to boot.log (for example `U3MP online: signed in, srv OK, session live`, or the step that failed), so a "can't log in" report can be diagnosed from one log. No server address, account name or password is ever written.

## Crashes

- **Lobby crash fixed** (a crash reading address 0x38 at 0xbd6a2f, or the "pMgr != NULL" assert, while entering or leaving the lobby): the lobby now waits a frame when your character's model or the camera is not ready yet, instead of creating the character too early.

## Launcher

- **Updates are much faster.** After a launcher update, only the multiplayer files that changed are rebuilt: the update starts from a copy of your current files (a minute or less) instead of rebuilding everything (hours).
- **Uncharted 3 waits for its multiplayer update.** While an update is waiting or running, a gold bar under the game cards shows its progress and PLAY shows UPDATING... for Uncharted 3 (Uncharted 1 and 2 can still be played). PLAY unlocks when it finishes.
- **Version numbers in the title bar** of the launcher and of the game window: Launcher Version and Recipe Version.
