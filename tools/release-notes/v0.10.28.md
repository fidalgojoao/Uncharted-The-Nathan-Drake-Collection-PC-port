**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 online (multiplayer build v7.88)

- **Fewer "No connection found" errors on Play Online.** After you sign in, the game downloads your online profile from the server. If that download failed or was still running, Play Online stopped at once with "No connection found", and it never tried again until you signed in from scratch. Now the download is retried by itself, and Play Online waits on the Connecting / "Syncing live data" screen (up to 30 seconds) while it finishes, as on PS3.
- If it still fails, your boot.log now says exactly why: please send it.

## Launcher

- **Create an RPCN account in the launcher.** Uncharted 3 Multiplayer > RPCN Setup > "No RPCN account yet? Create one": pick a username and password, type your email, and press CREATE ACCOUNT. RPCN emails you a token: type it in RPCN Token and press CONNECT. "Email my token again" sends it once more if it does not arrive.

Known: while Uncharted 3 runs, the launcher is signed out of RPCN (the game signs in with your account), so adding friends from the launcher waits until the game is closed.

Still not working: the Invites tab stays empty (join through the friend list instead).
