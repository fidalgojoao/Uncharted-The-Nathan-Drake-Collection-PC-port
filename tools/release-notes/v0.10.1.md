**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. Your multiplayer import updates itself in the background (the multiplayer image is relinked in seconds). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online play works again

- **RPCN "not available, no DNS".** The multiplayer helper of 0.9.x (v7.55) could not use the network at all, so signing in failed for everyone and the server check never sent anything. It was not your firewall or your internet. Multiplayer build **v7.56** fixes it: the server check, the sign-in and the online lobby work again.
- **No more LAN profile page on Play Online.** On a new install, Play Online asked you to make a LAN profile first. It now uses your RPCN account: the first profile is made from your account name, and your online profile (name, level, prestige icon, stats and clan tag) is loaded from the server, as before.

## Import fixes (from players' logs)

- **Updates that ended with "registry-final" or "wall-emblem" failed.** On the Game of the Year disc, the map list sources extracted by an older version were missing files, and the Airstrip emblem step refused when the map list had been rewritten. Both now finish. If your last update reported these, press UPDATE.
