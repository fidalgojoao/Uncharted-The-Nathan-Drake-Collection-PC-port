**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## New launcher look

- **A new launcher window** (Qt): dark, with the Collection's yellow accent, rounded buttons, switches, choices and boxes. The picture on top stays.
- **Every tab is split into sections**, side by side in two columns. Advanced is split into Game, Extra quality, Unlocks and Launcher; its shortcut, Steam, folder and update buttons moved to a new **Tools** tab.
- **Multiplayer tabs:**
  - your PS3 game, server and storage on the left, **RPCN** on the right;
  - the RPCN account part folds away with the arrow;
  - Add friend sits above the friends list, with its button inside the box;
  - the import shows a progress bar (Open import log has all the details);
  - shorter explanations everywhere.
- **Controls:** mouse and controller settings are normal rows; Reset controls is next to the bindings.
- **The window reopens at the size and place you left it.**
- Nothing changed in what the settings do. If the new window cannot start on your PC, the launcher opens the previous window instead. The per-game shortcuts (Steam, desktop) start the games as before.

## Multiplayer imports

- **The decrypted PS3 EBOOT.elf is now required** for the Uncharted 3 and Uncharted 2 imports. Decrypt it in RPCS3 first: Utilities > Decrypt PS3 Binaries, then pick your game's `USRDIR/EBOOT.BIN`. The `EBOOT.elf` it writes next to it is found by itself.
- If you already imported without it, your multiplayer keeps working: an automatic update just waits until the EBOOT.elf is there.
