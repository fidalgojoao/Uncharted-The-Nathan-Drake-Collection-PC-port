**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (the Uncharted 3 multiplayer image is relinked in a few seconds). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer (build v7.62)

- **Crash fixed: the game could close while characters loaded** (seen in a lobby while two skins loaded; boot.log ended with `Fault: read access to 0000000000000004 at guest offset 0xe99047`). The game read a character part slot twice while its loader thread could empty it in between; it now keeps the part it already checked. The online helper is unchanged from v7.61.
- **The Asia Game of the Year edition (BCAS25014) works too**, next to the EU (BCES01670), UK (BCES01692) and US (BCUS99086) editions. Its 1.10 and 1.19 EBOOTs and update are byte-identical to the EU ones, and its game files are identical apart from the speech languages (the import uses English).

## Uncharted 2 multiplayer (in development)

The Uncharted 2 **MULTIPLAYER** tab now shows the new Uncharted 2 multiplayer restoration, built on the same design as Uncharted 3's. Some parts do not work yet; it is a preview.

- **The tab looks and works like Uncharted 3's**: your PS3 Uncharted 2 folder and map packs, IMPORT / RE-IMPORT, Cancel, the import log, a progress bar and the step being done.
- **The import runs in steps** (files from the PS3 copy, map menu images, map spawn data, the server data module, registries, text). After a launcher update only the steps that changed are redone, by themselves in the background; the gold bar under the game cards shows it, and Uncharted 2 can be played when it finishes (Uncharted 1 and 3 meanwhile). Closing the launcher does not stop an import.
- **RPCN Setup on the Uncharted 2 tab too**: the same account and sign-in as on Uncharted 3's tab (one session for both). Uncharted 2 itself does not use RPCN yet; its multiplayer is LAN only for now.
- The earlier Uncharted 2 LAN preview (the co-op and competitive LAN games, two windows on one PC) is replaced by this restoration.
