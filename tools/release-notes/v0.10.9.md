**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. The changed maps are rebuilt in the background; nothing is imported again. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Maps

- **7 more maps are now rebuilt exactly like the reference build**, end to end: Chateau, Chateau shade, the two Borneo co-op maps (approach and stage), London streets, and both London train maps.
  - Borneo co-op: the grass material fix now matches the original and also reaches the approach map.
  - London train: the sun brightness value is now written exactly.
  - Chateau and London streets: leftover backups from earlier imports no longer make the colour fix skip these maps.
- 12 maps still differ from the reference. Exact rebuilds for all of them have been found and are being added to the import.
