**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (or let it install by itself: LAUNCHER tab, Automatic). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 multiplayer map lighting (overlay recipe 1.11.0)

Thanks to 33martyr for these fixes. After the update, the launcher reapplies the multiplayer import by itself to apply them.

- **Sunlight no longer floods indoor areas.** Some invisible sun blockers were missing from the sun's shadow pass. In the London Underground library, this gave a bright cream floor, dark walls and hard light shafts. Fixed on 19 multiplayer and co-op maps, including London Underground, Airstrip, Fort, Highrise and Yemen.
- **Fort:** the stone stairs no longer look like chrome. The cream plaster walls use the PS3 colour, and a few surfaces lost a metallic shine that the PS3 version doesn't have.

Still to do: some Fort plaster panels are still brighter than on PS3.
