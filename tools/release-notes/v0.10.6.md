**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. The multiplayer image is relinked and the four Monastery co-op maps are rebuilt in the background; nothing is imported again. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Fixes

- **Co-op Monastery chapter 3: crash to the desktop.** Four Monastery co-op maps (church, cloister, courtyard, front) were rebuilt slightly differently from the reference, and one of their collision meshes made the game's physics stop. All four now rebuild exactly like the reference.
- **Online Custom Game: crash when you press Start Game.** The match lost its connection to the online service when the Custom Game page opened. It stays connected now, and the match goes on to the countdown.
- **Backing out of a search (Find Game, co-op adventure and others)**: the menu kept going back by itself (a burst of clicks), got stuck, or crashed, and Custom Game would not start afterwards. Backing out now returns to the lobby normally.

## Known

- Leaving a search can still ask you to confirm twice.
