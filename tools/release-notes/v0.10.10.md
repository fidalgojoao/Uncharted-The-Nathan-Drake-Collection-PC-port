**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. The changed files are rebuilt in the background; nothing is imported again. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Every map is now exact

- **All 325 multiplayer and co-op maps are now rebuilt exactly like the reference build**, byte for byte. The last 12 are Cave (and its stage), Airport co-op 2, both Syria co-op areas, Fort co-op tower, Fort, Graveyard stage, Highrise, Museum, Sanctuary upper stage and Shambhala.
- **The particle effects of Cave, Gompa and Shambhala** (the 1.19 effects, including the Sanctuary water splashes) are now rebuilt from your own game files too, using the recovered original converter.
- In a full test import, every file the game loads matched the reference build: 14,800 of 14,800.
