**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## The game data is downloaded for you

The multiplayer import needs the PS3 game's online "game data" (`BCES01670DATA2`): the network table, the Lab maps and the map packs. Until now you only had it if you had gone online in Uncharted 3 on RPCS3.

- **New field on the Multiplayer tab: Community server DNS.** It's the same address as on the in-game RPCN page. If you already filled in that page, it's picked up automatically.
- When your RPCS3 copy is missing or out of date, the import downloads the same files the PS3 game would, from the community server. They're decrypted with the keys from your own PS3 EBOOT and checked against the expected versions.
- **If the download doesn't work** (server down, or the files aren't served yet): nothing breaks. Uncharted 3 starts normally without multiplayer, and the launcher tries the download again every time you open it. Once the download works, the import finishes by itself in the background.
