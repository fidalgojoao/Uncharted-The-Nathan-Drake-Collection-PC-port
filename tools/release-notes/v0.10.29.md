**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Stay signed in to RPCN while you play (launcher + multiplayer build v7.89)

- **No more sign-out / sign-in when the game starts.** Before, the launcher signed out of RPCN when you pressed PLAY and the game signed in again by itself, so your friends saw you go offline and back online. Now the game uses the launcher's RPCN connection, and you stay signed in from the moment the launcher starts until you close it.
- **The launcher's friends list keeps working while you play**, and you can add or remove friends from the launcher during a match.
- **No effect on game speed:** only RPCN messages (sign-in, friends, invites, presence) take the short way through the launcher on your own PC. Match traffic between players and everything from the Uncharted 3 server still go directly from the game.
- If you close the launcher while playing, the game notices and signs in to RPCN by itself after a few seconds.

Keep the launcher open while you play for this to work.
