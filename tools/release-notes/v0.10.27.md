**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 online (multiplayer build v7.87)

- **Online Custom Games start.** Before, the map loaded and then the game dropped back to the lobby: your character was never created online, so the game left the match. It is now created when the match starts, as on PS3, and your character shows on the map's background while it loads.
- **Your party follows you into an online Custom Game** (new, needs a test). When the party leader starts, party members on the PC version join the match room by themselves and start together with the leader. If a member does not follow within 10 seconds, the leader starts without them instead of waiting.
- Players on RPCS3 or PS3 do not follow into a Custom Game yet.

Please send your boot.log (from every PC in the party) if a Custom Game does not start for everyone.

Still not working: the Invites tab stays empty (join through the friend list instead).
