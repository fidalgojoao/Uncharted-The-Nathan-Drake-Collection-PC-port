**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 online (multiplayer build v7.77)

- **Fewer "No connection found" when choosing Play Online.** Since 0.10.23 a failed server sign-in is tried again in the background, but Play Online did not wait for those retries and showed "No connection found" while one was still coming. It now waits for them (up to about two and a half minutes).
- **Invites stay in the Invites tab while it refreshes.** Opening the tab emptied the list until the server answered, and the list did not redraw afterwards.
- **More detail in boot.log** about the Invites tab, to finish the invite list fix.

Still not working: kicking a player and promoting a party leader; some invites still do not show in the Invites tab, and long names can be cut off in the invite message.
