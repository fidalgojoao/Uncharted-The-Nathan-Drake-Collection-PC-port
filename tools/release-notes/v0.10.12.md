**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Launcher

- **RPCN Setup on the Multiplayer tab** (Uncharted 3 selected): enter the DNS server, your RPCN username, password and token once, and the launcher signs you in by itself every time it opens.
  - Two status dots: **DNS** (the server answered) and **Account** (signed in, or the exact problem: wrong password, wrong token, already signed in elsewhere...).
  - **Friends**: your friends list with who is online, add a friend, accept or decline requests, cancel or remove.
  - The password and token are stored encrypted for your Windows user only.
  - While Uncharted 3 runs, the launcher signs itself out (RPCN allows one sign-in per account) and signs back in when you quit.
  - For now the game still uses its own in-game RPCN settings; a later update makes it use these.
