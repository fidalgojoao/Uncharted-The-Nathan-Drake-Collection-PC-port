**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY (the multiplayer image is relinked in a few seconds). **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Online (multiplayer helper v7.58)

- **"No connection found" while signed in fixed.** A reconnect to RPCN reset your online profile and redid the whole server login, so Play Online could fail for a few seconds even though the status said the session was live. A reconnect now keeps your profile and session.
- **Play Online waits for a sign-in in progress** (up to 30 s) instead of failing at once.
- **Automatic reconnect:** if the connection drops, the game signs in again by itself after a few seconds.
- **More robust networking on Windows:** the helper loads its network functions directly from Windows instead of through the game runtime.
