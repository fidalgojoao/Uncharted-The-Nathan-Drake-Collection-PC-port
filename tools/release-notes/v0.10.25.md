**Uncharted: The Nathan Drake Collection** PC port with the **Uncharted 3 multiplayer restoration** (beta).
Based on BilalReloaded's PC port (credits in the README).

**Already on 0.9.x or 0.10.x:** press **UPDATE** next to PLAY. **New install:** extract the zip into an empty folder and start `Uncharted Launcher.exe`.

No game files are included. Everything game-derived is made on your PC from your own PS3 and PS4 copies.

## Uncharted 3 online (multiplayer build v7.79)

New, not tested in a full session yet: please send your boot.log if something here does not work.

- **Party menu online: Kick, Promote to party leader and Report Player.** Select a party member in the party list. Kick and Promote are offered to the party leader; Report Player shows the same reasons as on PS3 and sends the report to the server.
- **Statistics of other players** now show their real profile from the server (it may take a moment to load), instead of empty values.
- **Party members' rank badges** in the party list and in the matchmaking players list come from their profiles (other players always showed rank 1 before).
- **Party matchmaking:** when the party leader starts Find Game and enters a match room, the party members now follow into the same room, as on PS3.
- **boot.log** lists every matchmaking step ("matchmaking: ..."), to fix what is left quickly.

Still not working: the Invites tab stays empty (join through the friend list instead); long names can be cut off in the invite message.
